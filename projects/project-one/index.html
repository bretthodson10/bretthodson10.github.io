Carbon Fiber Bike Lug – VAMOS Lab at Oregon State

Created a custom bottom bracket composite bike lug using a prototype curing method.

Tools Fusion 360, 3D Printing, OnShape Date May 2026

The problem

The Sports Product Development Club (SPD) at Oregon State sought to make a full bike frame out of carbon fiber, but traditional layup processes like vaccum bagging would not be able to make an adequate bike lug to connect frame tubes together and distribute stresses throughout the joints.

What I did

I worked alongside VAMOS Lab MS student Ben Holmgren who invented a prototype curing method which could provide forces significantly higher than atmospheric pressure during the curing process (compared to vaccum bagging). Ben taught me how to perform surface modeling processes in Fusion 360 to take an existing CAD model and develop inner and outer tooling shells to protect the carbon fiber during the curing process. These tools were 3D printed in the lab using PLA.
